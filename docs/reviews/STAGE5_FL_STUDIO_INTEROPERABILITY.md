### Contamination and retest resolution

An initial inspection showed a short F# and a short D in the Chords piano roll. The product owner identified both as accidental manual Piano Roll entries. A fresh blank-project re-import of the unchanged fixture, with the same settings and no editing before inspection, showed neither note; playback through bar 9 had no stuck, missing, or unexpected audible notes. The clean re-import above is the authoritative acceptance run, and the accidental entries are not an interoperability defect.
